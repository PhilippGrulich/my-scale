Which little scale feels like yours?

Three takes on a cute talking scale, in the app's green. Click the one you like best (or tell me what to mix).

A · Round "Pebble"

A round scale whose display is its face. Tiny stubby feet. Very simple, reads well even small (app icon, widget, Watch).

B · Square "Digi"

A classic bathroom scale with a little LCD for eyes and waving arms. More "gadget buddy", can show numbers in its display.

C · Squishy "Mochi"

A soft, jelly-like scale with an antenna and the weight on its tummy. The most cuddly and expressive, easiest to animate squish and bounce.
